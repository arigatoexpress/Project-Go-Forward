Why Texas Home Outlet

Texas Home Outlet is the premiere retailer of quality manufactured homes in Huffman and surrounding areas in Texas. Our one-stop shop focuses on customer service, quality construction and client satisfaction. Let us help you choose a home that fits your unique wants and needs, offer assistance with financing and ensure you make the most of your housing dollar.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

The manufactured homes sold at Texas Home Outlet are built with the same materials used in so-called “stick-built” homes. However, each home from Texas Home Outlet is built in a controlled, factory environment with no concerns about weather slowing down the process or exposing your new home to weather damage.

AFFORDABILITY

The manufactured homes and modular Homes offered for sale at Texas Home Outlet can be much more affordable than site-built homes because of the factory construction process. A factory built home saves you money. The workers building your new home are on the job, rain or shine, putting you in your new home faster, and for far less money.

AMENITIES

Almost any custom amenities available in stick-built homes are available in the modern manufactured homes available at Texas Home Outlet. Custom kitchens, fireplaces and upgraded bathrooms are available on many of our models.
