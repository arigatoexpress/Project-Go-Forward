### What's the difference between a manufactured home, a mobile home, and a modular home?
"Mobile home" is the older term for factory-built homes made **before June 15, 1976**.
Homes built after that date to the federal **HUD Code** are called **manufactured
homes** — they meet national standards for construction, strength, energy efficiency,
and fire safety. **Modular homes** are also factory-built but assembled to local/state
building codes (like a site-built home). Texas Home Outlet sells new **manufactured
homes** in single- and double-section (and multi-section) floor plans.

### How does financing work for a manufactured home?
Several options are commonly available depending on whether you own or are buying land:
- **Chattel (home-only) loans** — for the home itself when it's on leased land or in a
  community. Typically faster, with shorter terms.
- **Land-and-home / mortgage loans** — when the home and land are financed together;
  may qualify for **FHA Title I/II**, **VA**, or **USDA** programs.
- **In-house / lender-partner programs** for a range of credit profiles.

### What warranty comes with a new manufactured home?
New manufactured homes carry a **manufacturer's warranty** on the home and its major
systems, and many components (appliances, HVAC, roofing) carry their own
manufacturer warranties. In Texas, manufactured housing is regulated by the **Texas
Department of Housing and Community Affairs (TDHCA)**. We'll provide the specific
warranty documents for the home you choose. *[THO: confirm warranty length/terms by
