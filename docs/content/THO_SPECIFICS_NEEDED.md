# THO Business Specifics Needed — Content Questionnaire

> **Owner:** Mira (research/ops lane) · **Status:** Awaiting Ari's answers
> **Last updated:** 2026-06-15

## Purpose

The THO website has several pages that are either 404s (About, FAQ) or thin on content (Financing). To build trust and rank locally, these pages need **real business specifics** — not generic filler. This questionnaire captures what we need from Ari.

Once answered, Claude/Kimi can build the actual pages.

---

## 1. Business Identity & Story

- [ ] **Founding story:** When did Texas Home Outlet start? Why? What problem were you solving?
- [ ] **Who is Ari?** What's your background? (e.g., "3rd generation in manufactured housing", "former contractor", etc.)
- [ ] **Team:** How many people work at THO? Names/roles? (Even 2–3 people with photos builds massive trust.)
- [ ] **Mission/values:** What makes THO different from Tejas Homes or Oakwood Homes? (Price? Service? Selection? Speed?)
- [ ] **Service area:** Exact cities/counties you serve. (We'll use this for city pages + GBP service area.)

## 2. Financing

- [ ] **Financing partners:** Which lenders do you work with? (e.g., Vanderbilt, 21st Mortgage, Triad, local credit unions?)
- [ ] **Down payment ranges:** What's typical? (e.g., "$0 down with approved credit" or "5–10% down")
- [ ] **Credit requirements:** Do you work with bad credit? What's the minimum score? Is there a co-signer option?
- [ ] **Land-home packages:** Do you offer these? How do they work? (This is a huge differentiator — Tejas mentions it prominently.)
- [ ] **Monthly payment examples:** Can you share 2–3 real examples? (e.g., "$450/mo for a 3BR/2BA on your land")
- [ ] **Approval timeline:** How fast can someone get approved? (Same day? 48 hours?)

## 3. Home Delivery & Setup

- [ ] **What's included in delivery?** (Transport, setup, skirting, steps, AC, tie-downs?)
- [ ] **Delivery radius:** How far will you deliver? (e.g., 100 miles from Huffman?)
- [ ] **Delivery timeline:** From purchase to move-in, how long? (2 weeks? 6 weeks?)
- [ ] **Setup crew:** Is it THO employees or subcontractors? (Employees = trust signal.)
- [ ] **Permitting:** Do you handle permits, or is that on the buyer?

## 4. Warranty & After-Sale Support

- [ ] **Manufacturer warranties:** What do the manufacturers cover, and for how long? (e.g., "1-year full, 5-year structural")
- [ ] **THO warranty:** Do you offer anything beyond the manufacturer warranty? (e.g., 30-day satisfaction guarantee?)
- [ ] **Service after the sale:** If something breaks in month 3, who do they call? What's the process?
- [ ] **Maintenance resources:** Do you provide a homeowner maintenance guide or checklist?

## 5. Manufacturers & Inventory

- [ ] **Which manufacturers do you carry?** (TRU, Clayton, Cavco, Champion, Fleetwood, Skyline, others?)
- [ ] **Exclusive brands?** Do you carry any brands that competitors in the area don't?
- [ ] **Price range:** What's the range for new homes? (e.g., "$40K–$120K")
- [ ] **Pre-owned inventory:** Do you sell pre-owned? How many typically in stock?
- [ ] **Customization:** Can buyers customize floor plans? What's the process/timeline?

## 6. Hours & Contact

- [ ] **Business hours:** Exact days and times (e.g., Mon–Sat 9am–6pm, Sun 12pm–5pm)
- [ ] **Walk-ins vs appointments:** Do you accept walk-ins, or appointment only?
- [ ] **Phone number:** Primary contact number
- [ ] **Physical address:** Full street address for the lot
- [ ] **Email:** General contact email
- [ ] **Social media:** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok links (if active)

## 7. Customer Proof

- [ ] **Testimonials:** Can you share 3–5 real customer stories? (First name, city, what they bought, what they loved.)
- [ ] **Photos:** Do you have photos of happy customers in front of their new home? (With permission to use.)
- [ ] **Video testimonials:** Any video? (Even phone-recorded is fine — authenticity > production value.)
- [ ] **Google reviews:** Can you ask 5 recent customers to leave a Google review this week? (We'll provide a direct link.)

You don't need to write perfect copy — bullet points and rough answers are fine. Claude/Kimi will turn them into polished page content. The important thing is **accuracy**. Wrong specifics are worse than no specifics.

Reply in this doc, in a separate message, or voice memo — whatever's fastest.
