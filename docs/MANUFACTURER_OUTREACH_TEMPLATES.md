## Tips for Success

1. **Call First, Email Second** - Phone calls get faster responses
2. **Mention Your Dealer Number** - Shows legitimacy
3. **Be Specific** - List exact models you need
4. **Set Deadline** - "Need by end of week for launch"
5. **Follow Up** - Every 2-3 days until received
6. **Build Relationship** - Get direct contact for future requests

---

## What to Do With Received Assets

1. **Save Originals** - Keep in `data/manufacturer_assets/[manufacturer]/`
2. **Optimize for Web** - Resize to max 1920px width
3. **Upload to CDN** - If you have your own storage
4. **Update asset_scraper.py** - Add new image URLs
5. **Update Firestore** - Link images to inventory items
6. **Test** - Verify images load properly on AI platform
