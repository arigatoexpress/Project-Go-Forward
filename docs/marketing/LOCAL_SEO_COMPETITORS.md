> **Owner:** Mira (research/ops lane) · **Status:** Draft for Ari review

## Methodology

Analyzed the local pack and organic results for "manufactured homes in {city} TX" across THO's four target markets: **Huffman, Humble, Conroe, Baytown**. Focused on what competitors have that THO must match or beat.

### Analysis
- Humble is **mostly mobile-home parks**, not dealers. THO has a clear positioning advantage as a *dealer*.
- The search intent for "manufactured homes Humble TX" is split between "buy a home" and "rent a lot" — THO should target the buyer intent explicitly.

### What THO Must Do to Win Humble
1. **Landing page** `/humble-tx` — target "buy manufactured homes near Humble" (not rent)
2. **Schema:** `LocalBusiness` + `Product` markup on inventory pages
3. **Content:** FAQ addressing "buying vs renting" in the Humble area
