## Priority 1 — Claim Immediately (Week 1)

These are the highest-impact citations. Do them first.

### 1. Google Business Profile
- **URL:** https://business.google.com/
- **Why:** #1 local ranking factor. Drives the Knowledge Panel, Maps pin, and "near me" results.
- **Action:** Claim, verify (postcard/phone), complete every field. Add 10+ photos (lot, homes, team, signage). Enable messaging. Write a keyword-rich description (≤750 chars): *"Texas Home Outlet is Huffman's premier manufactured and modular home dealer, serving Humble, Conroe, Baytown, and the greater Houston area…"*
- **Posts:** Publish 1/week — new inventory, promotions, customer stories.
- **Reviews:** Reply to every review within 24h. Seed 5 initial reviews from recent customers.

### 2. Bing Places for Business
- **URL:** https://www.bingplaces.com/
- **Why:** Powers Bing Maps + DuckDuckGo local results. Low competition = easy wins.
- **Action:** Same NAP, same photos, same categories as GBP.

### 3. Apple Maps / Maps Connect
- **URL:** https://mapsconnect.apple.com/
- **Why:** Powers Siri + Apple Maps. Often auto-generated from other sources but verify for accuracy.
- **Action:** Claim, verify, match NAP exactly.

### 4. Yelp
- **URL:** https://biz.yelp.com/
- **Why:** High domain authority. Often ranks on page 1 for branded + category searches.
- **Action:** Claim, add photos, respond to reviews. Don't solicit reviews (Yelp penalizes), but let happy customers know you're on Yelp.

## Priority 2 — Industry-Specific Directories (Week 2)

These carry strong topical relevance for manufactured housing.

### 5. MHVillage
- **URL:** https://www.mhvillage.com/dealers/tx
- **Why:** The #1 marketplace for manufactured homes. Dealer listings rank well in Google.
- **Action:** Create dealer profile, link to inventory pages, list available homes.

### 6. ManufacturedHomes.com
- **URL:** https://www.manufacturedhomes.com/dealers/
- **Why:** Large national directory. Already has a Humble, TX category page.
- **Action:** Claim or create listing. Ensure NAP matches.

### 7. Zillow — Mobile/Manufactured Section
- **URL:** https://www.zillow.com/houston-tx/mobile/
- **Why:** Massive traffic. THO's inventory should appear here for local searches.
- **Action:** List available homes via Zillow Rental Manager or feed integration.

### 8. BBB (Better Business Bureau)
- **URL:** https://www.bbb.org/us/tx/humble/category/manufactured-home-manufacturers
- **Why:** Trust signal. BBB pages rank on page 1 for dealer searches.
- **Action:** Apply for accreditation (if desired) or at minimum claim the free listing.

### 9. Texas Manufactured Housing Association (TMHA)
- **URL:** https://www.texasmha.com/
- **Why:** Industry association membership = credibility + backlink from .org domain.
- **Action:** Join TMHA, get listed in their member directory.

## NAP Consistency Checklist

After claiming all listings, audit for consistency:

- [ ] Business name is **exactly** "Texas Home Outlet" everywhere (no "THO", no "Texas Home Outlets")
- [ ] Street address format matches (e.g., "FM 2100" vs "Farm-to-Market 2100" — pick one)
- [ ] Phone number format is consistent (e.g., (281) 555-1234 vs 281-555-1234 — pick one)
- [ ] Website URL uses **www.texashomeoutlet.com** (not non-www, not http)
- [ ] Category is "Manufactured Home Dealer" (not just "Home Dealer" or "Real Estate")
- [ ] Hours match across all platforms
- [ ] No duplicate listings on any platform (merge if found)

### Audit Tools
- **BrightLocal** ($39/mo) — best for citation audit + cleanup
- **Whitespark** ($30/mo) — good alternative for local citation building
- **Manual Google search** — search "Texas Home Outlet" + "Huffman" and check every result

## Ongoing Maintenance

- **Monthly:** Check for new reviews on GBP, Yelp, Facebook. Respond within 24h.
- **Quarterly:** Re-audit NAP consistency. Update hours if they change.
- **On new inventory:** Post to GBP, MHVillage, Zillow, Facebook.
- **On new manufacturer relationship:** Submit to their dealer locator immediately.
