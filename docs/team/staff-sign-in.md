# How to sign in

# 1. Open this page

https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/staff

Bookmark it on your phone and on the office computer.

# 2. Type your work email

# 3. Tap Email me a sign-in link

# 4. Open the email and tap Sign me in

You are in.

The email also shows a 6-digit code. Use it only if the button does not open. It works for 10 minutes.

# If it does not work

Check spam.

Tap the button again.

If nothing comes, ask a teammate who can sign in to add your email.

That link expired? Tap **Email me a new sign-in link**.

# You stay signed in

You can close the page. This phone or computer stays signed in.

On a shared computer, tap **Sign out**.

# Backup

**Use backup PIN** is the small link under the email box. Use it only if email is not working and someone gave you the PIN.

Do not text or email your code or PIN to anyone.
