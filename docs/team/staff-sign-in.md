# How to sign in

Use this page: **https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/staff**

Bookmark it on your phone and on the office computer. Always use the address that starts with **www**. The lock button on the public site opens the same sign-in.

## Sign in

1. Open the bookmark.
2. Type your work email. That is usually a `@texashomeoutlet.com` address.
3. Tap **Email me a sign-in link**.
4. Open the email from Texas Home Outlet. Check the inbox first, then spam.
5. Tap **Sign me in**.
6. On the next page, tap **Sign me in** again.

You are in. The Team, Photos, Documents, and CRM buttons show up in the menu.

The email also has a 6-digit code. Use it only if the button does not open. Type the code on the sign-in page and tap **Verify**. The code works once and expires in 10 minutes.

## If it does not work

- **No email after a couple of minutes.** Check spam. Tap **Resend code** once. If it still does not come, your email may not be on the team list. Ask a teammate who can already sign in to open **Team** and add you.
- **The page says email sign-in is not turned on.** The office email sender is not set up yet. Use **Use backup PIN** if you have the shared PIN, and ask the owner to finish email setup.
- **The button in the email opens the wrong site.** Copy this address into the browser yourself: `https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/staff`. Then type the 6-digit code from the email.
- **You used to sign in with a fingerprint or passkey and it stopped working.** That is expected if the passkey was saved on the old website. You do not need it. Use the email steps above.
- **Someone should no longer have access.** Sign in, open **Team**, and block their email. That stops their email sign-in. If they also knew the shared backup PIN, ask the owner to change that PIN.

Do not text or email your code or PIN to anyone, including support.

## For someone who manages the team

Open **Team** after you sign in.

- Company emails (`@texashomeoutlet.com`) can sign in unless you block them.
- Tap **Add** to let in an email from another company, such as a bookkeeper.
- Tap **Block** to stop someone. Tap **Unblock** to allow them again.
- Owner emails cannot be blocked from this page.
