# Things that trip up staff

This page is for people who work at Texas Home Outlet. You do not need tech skills.

The website is https://www.texashomeoutlet.com

Each note has one clear step. If something still looks wrong, take a screenshot and ask your manager.

Other guides: [How to sign in](staff-sign-in.md), [How to add photos](photo-upload-guide.md), [How to use Ad Studio](ad-studio-guide.md).

## Do these five first

### Prices

Homes show **Call for Price** until someone types a sale price.

**Do:** If the owner or operator says a price can go on the site, sign in, tap **Manage Homes**, pick the home, and type the number in **Price on the website**. Then save.

**Don't:** Leave that box blank and expect a dollar amount to show. A blank price shows Call for Price.

Ask the owner or operator which prices can show. A manager who is not the owner or operator cannot approve a public sale price.

### Ad Studio: if Prepare Draft fails

**Do:** If you see **The draft wasn't saved. Please try again.**, press **Prepare Draft** one more time.

The preview stays open, so your words are still there.

### Ad Studio: Google Ads stays off

Google Ads is the paid search tab. It is off on purpose.

**Do:** Leave the Google Ads tab alone.

**Don't:** Press the buttons on that tab. Those ads cost money when they run, and they are meant to stay off.

This tab cannot turn the ads on by itself.

### Signing in

**Do:** Go to https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/staff. Use the email link, or the newest 6-digit code in that email. If email is down and someone gave you the backup PIN, use **Use backup PIN**.

**Don't:** Tap **Resend code** over and over. Extra taps will not make the email arrive faster. Use the newest code if you asked more than once.

**Don't:** Look for **Save sign-in on this phone**. Passkey sign-in is turned off, so that button stays hidden. If you ever see it, leave it alone and use the email link.

### Homes and photos: real photos only

Used homes show only real photos of that home on the lot. Factory catalog pictures stay hidden. A floor-plan drawing shows as a floor plan, not as a photo of the home.

**Do:** Upload real photos taken on the lot.

**Don't:** Upload a floor plan and expect it to show as a photo.

## The rest, by screen

### Ad Studio

**Do:** Read the preview, then press **Prepare Draft** only when you want a draft on this screen.

Ad Studio never posts by itself. The screen says **Nothing is posted.** A draft is only a draft. TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook do not get it until a person posts it there by hand.

**Do:** Copy the words, or download the picture, before you sign out.

A prepared draft stays on this browser while you stay signed in. Signing out removes it. Nothing is posted.

### Signing in

**Do:** If everyone is signed out at the same time, sign in again.

That is a safety sign-out for the whole team, not a broken page. It happened on 2026-10-09.

### Homes and photos

**Do:** After you sign in, tap **Manage Homes** to change a home. Tap **Photos** (the camera button) to add pictures.

There is no menu button named Inventory. **Homes for Sale** is the public page customers see. It is not the screen where you edit a home.

You can also open these pages directly:

- https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/manage-inventory
- https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/photos

**Do:** If you upload a photo on a used home and it does not show, ask your manager.

Photo upload on a used home has not been tested on the live site yet.

### Contracts

**Do:** On a deal, press **Review & Send for Signature** to see a preview. That button only shows a preview.

If the panel says **E-signing isn't turned on yet, so nothing can be sent.**, print the packet and sign in person.

**Don't:** Send anything to a buyer on your own. Nothing goes to a buyer unless you tick the hand-check box and press **Send to** the buyer's email. Ask the owner or operator before you send anything. A manager who is not the owner or operator cannot approve that send.

**Do:** Read the contract packet before you print it.

The escrow payment prints on the papers. The Insurance Escrow box ticks itself when insurance is part of that escrow.

**Don't:** Hand the papers over if any line does not match the deal sheet. Stop and ask your manager.

### New Deal form

**Do:** If the New Deal form shows an error that names a field, ask your manager.

The message starts with "Could not save this deal" and names the field the site does not know. Nothing was saved. The deal is not in the list.

### Leads

**Do:** Open the new-lead email and read which home the customer asked about.

The email has a line that starts with **Interested in**. If they did not name a home, the line says **General inquiry**.

### Links

**Do:** Keep using an old home link you already shared. It still opens that home.

A link that contains `?home=` opens that home's page. A link that contains `/inventory-detail/` opens that home too.

On a phone, **Call**, **Text**, and **Apply** show under the home name.

The same page also shows **Call**, **Text**, and **Apply for financing** again further down. That extra set is still there.
