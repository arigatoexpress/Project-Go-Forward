# How to Use Ad Studio

**For everyone on the sales team. No Google Ads experience required.**

Ad Studio helps you write a social post or check the Google search ad.
It does not post anything, and it does not spend money.

The website is https://www.texashomeoutlet.com

---

## Open it

1. Go to **https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/staff** and sign in.
   See [How to sign in](staff-sign-in.md).
2. Open **Ad Studio** for social posts: https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/studio
3. Open **Google Ads** for the search ad: https://www.texashomeoutlet.com/ad-studio

You can also use the **Google Ads** button inside Ad Studio.

---

## Social posts (Create Ad)

Use this when you want words, a picture, or a slideshow for TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook.

1. Pick the presenter, the language, where the post will go, and the topic.
2. Optional: choose a home from inventory. Leave the price as **Call for Price** unless the owner or operator has approved a number. A manager who is not the owner or operator cannot approve a public sale price.
3. Optional: write the opening line yourself. Leave it blank and the screen will write one.
4. Press **Generate Script**. The words show on the phone preview. Nothing is posted.
5. Read it. Words in **[brackets]** are camera notes. Do not read them out loud.
6. If you want a picture, a spoken version, or an AI video, the screen asks you to confirm first. Those can use a paid AI tool. They still do not post the ad.
7. Press **Prepare Draft** only after you have read the preview. That saves the draft on this screen. It does not send it to TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook.

### Drafts on this browser

A prepared draft stays on this browser while you stay signed in.
Signing out removes it. Copy the words or download the picture before you sign out.

To put the post on TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook, a person still has to post it there by hand.

## Google Ads

This screen is for one search ad around the Huffman showroom. You do not build a new campaign from scratch.

1. **Create.** In a sentence or two, say who should see the ad and what you want them to do. Your note stays on this screen. It is not the ad text.
2. Press **Check this ad**. That checks the ad on our website. It does not send anything to Google and does not spend money.
3. **Preview.** Read both ads, the area around the showroom, and the budget. Check the box when you have read them.
4. **Launch turned off.** Only the owner can do this, with the owner passkey. It creates the campaign in Google Ads **turned off**. It does not turn ads on, change billing, or spend money.

The screen asks "Continue?" before that launch. If you are not sure, press Cancel.

### What you cannot do here

- Turn ads on.
- Change the budget.
- Paste Google codes, tokens, or passwords. If the screen says the owner still has a setup step, stop and ask your manager.

Before ads can actually run, the Google Ads account has to be linked, billing has to be set up, tour bookings have to be counted, and the Google connection has to be finished. That work is not done on this screen. Ask your manager.

---

## If something fails

- A red message means it did not work. The ad was not posted and no money was spent.
- Press the retry button on that message, or refresh and try the same step again.
- If Google Ads says it is unavailable, press **Load again**. Nothing was sent to Google.
- If ideas or the numbers page fail, press **Refresh**. Those pages do not post anything.
- If you are stuck, take a screenshot and ask your manager. Do not paste any customer Social Security number or bank number into the chat.

---

## Prices

Public ads stay **Call for Price** unless the owner or operator has approved a number for that home. A manager who is not the owner or operator cannot approve a public sale price.
Do not type a dollar price into an ad on your own.
